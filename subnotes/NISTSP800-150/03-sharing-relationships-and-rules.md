# 03 - Establishing Sharing Relationships and Rules

## Setup Sequence (Section 3)
1. Define sharing goals/objectives.
2. Identify internal threat information sources.
3. Define scope of sharing activities.
4. Establish sharing rules.
5. Join one or more sharing communities.
6. Plan ongoing support (people, process, technology, funding).

## Internal Source Inventory
- Include network, host, SIEM, email, ticketing, incident, forensic, and monitoring sources.
- Track data quality (frequency, precision, accuracy), format, and access methods.
- Identify gaps to fill via new sensors/tools or external feeds.

## Sharing Rules Must Define
- What can be shared, with whom, and under what conditions.
- Redaction/sanitization requirements.
- Attribution permissions.
- Handling designations and enforcement procedures.

## Sensitivity and Privacy
- Account for PII, CUI, legal/contractual restrictions, and cross-border requirements.
- Prefer automated identification/redaction/anonymization where feasible.
- Include breach handling procedures for inadvertent disclosure.

## TLP (Traffic Light Protocol) Essentials
- **RED:** only within specific conversation/exchange.
- **AMBER:** limited to recipient organization and need-to-know.
- **GREEN:** limited community sharing, not public.
- **WHITE:** unrestricted disclosure.

## Community Selection Checks
- Relevance, actionability, timeliness, quality, and volume handling capacity.
- Governance model, membership rules, agreements, costs, and retention/disposal policies.
