# 05 - Scenarios and ISSMP Exam Angle

## Appendix A Scenario Themes
- Nation-state targeting of one sector.
- Campaign analysis across multiple organizations.
- DDoS campaign coordination with ISAC/ISPs/law enforcement.
- Targeted phishing against finance personnel.
- Business partner compromise and coordinated response.
- US-CERT indicator sharing with feedback loop.
- Retailer failure to share and resulting missed defense opportunity.

## Repeated Success Pattern
- Pre-established trust and agreements.
- Fast sharing of indicators and context.
- Joint analysis and coordinated response actions.
- Feedback loop to enrich and republish intelligence.

## Common Failure Pattern
- Weak trust/governance and delayed sharing.
- Poor quality controls on shared data.
- No clear handling designations or privacy controls.
- Inadequate staffing/funding for sustained participation.

## ISSMP High-Yield Memory Hook
- **Govern -> Share -> Validate -> Act -> Enrich -> Reshare**

## Exam-Oriented Management Actions
- Formalize sharing policy and legal/privacy alignment.
- Use risk-based, source-confidence-based indicator handling.
- Invest in automation where it reduces cycle time and error.
- Measure outcomes (time-to-detect/respond, false positives, partner utility).
