# 01 - Fundamentals and Threat Information Types (NIST SP 800-150)

## Purpose
- Provide guidance for sharing cyber threat information inside and across organizations.
- Improve detection, response, and risk-informed security decisions through collaboration.

## Core Concepts
- **Threat information:** data that helps identify, assess, monitor, or respond to threats.
- **Threat actors:** individuals or groups conducting malicious activity.
- **Goal of sharing:** acquire external intelligence and publish useful internal findings.

## Major Threat Information Types
- **Indicators:** artifacts/observables (IP, domain, URL, hash, email subject).
- **TTPs:** tactics, techniques, and procedures used by actors.
- **Security alerts/advisories:** timely notices on vulnerabilities, exploits, and issues.
- **Threat intelligence reports:** analyzed/enriched information for decisions.
- **Tool configurations:** practical detection/prevention configs (IDS rules, ACLs, etc.).

## ISSMP Takeaway
- Treat threat sharing as a management capability: governance + process + technical integration.
