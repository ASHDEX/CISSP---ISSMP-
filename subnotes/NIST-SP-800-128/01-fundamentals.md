# 01 - Fundamentals (NIST SP 800-128)

## Purpose
- Define Security-Focused Configuration Management (SecCM) for information systems.
- Integrate security into configuration management to reduce organizational risk.

## Key Definitions
- **Configuration Management (CM):** discipline to initialize, control, change, and monitor configurations.
- **SecCM:** management/control of secure configurations to enable security and risk management.
- **Configuration Item (CI):** managed entity (component/group/documentation/etc.) under configuration control.
- **Baseline Configuration:** formally approved configuration snapshot, changed only via control procedures.

## Why It Matters for ISSMP
- Connects governance, risk, and operations.
- Shows how security failures often come from misconfiguration, not only zero-days.
- Reinforces management accountability and repeatable processes.
